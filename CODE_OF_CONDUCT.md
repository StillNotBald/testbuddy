# Code of conduct

TestBuddy follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). Read the full text there; this file is a summary and does not replace it.

## In short

- **Be respectful and constructive.** Critique code and designs, not people.
- **Be welcoming.** People of any background, experience level or identity are welcome to contribute.
- **Assume good faith** until there is evidence otherwise, and say clearly when something is a problem.
- **Do not harass.** No personal attacks, trolling, insults, sexualised content, or unwanted sustained contact.
- **Do not publish others' private information** such as addresses, contact details or private messages, without their consent.
- **Keep discussion on topic** in issues and pull requests.

## Reporting a concern

Report conduct concerns privately. Open a private security advisory from the repository's **Security** tab with the title "Conduct report", and describe what happened, where, and who was involved. Maintainers will review it and respond in that thread. Do not post conduct concerns in public issues.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may ask for a change in behaviour, remove or edit comments, or restrict participation in the project, depending on severity. The Covenant's enforcement guidelines describe the levels in full.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, chats linked from the project) and when a person represents the project in public.
